# Ghost of Galm — Pixy Prerecorded Voicelines Recording Script

**Purpose:** recording script and file-placement guide for M7-B prerecorded wingman radio.

**Character basis:** Larry “Pixy” Foulke as defined by the canonical `docs/wingman/lorebook.md`.

## Recording / canon rules

- Current player address is primarily **“Kid.”** Do **not** use “Galm 1” or “Cipher” for the current player.
- **“Buddy” remains Cipher-associated** and is intentionally excluded from this recording script.
- Combat speech should usually be short, calm under pressure, experienced, informal, dry, and occasionally sarcastic.
- Praise should stay understated. Larry has flown with exceptional pilots before and should not worship the player.
- Mild profanity is acceptable when natural; do not make profanity the personality.
- Record **clean/dry voice**. Do not bake radio EQ, static, distortion, or compression into the source MP3; runtime Web Audio should add those effects.
- It is fine to record multiple takes of the same script. Keep the best performance for the suggested runtime filename.
- Elite/Boss lines below are **examples/placeholders**, not locked narrative canon. Revise them when encounter mechanics and story beats are finalized.
- M7-B should use cooldowns, weights, context filters, and silence; having many lines does not mean one must play for every event.
